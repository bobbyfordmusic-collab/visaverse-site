FAQ

How do I add songs or scripts?

Tap the "Add" tab, paste your text, give it a title, and tap "Add to Library."

How do I transfer my library to another device?

Go to the Import tab, tap "Export Library," then AirDrop the file to your other device. On that device, open the app, go to Import, and tap "Import Library."

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Vis-A-Verse works completely offline. No internet connection is needed after installation.

Can I use a foot pedal?

Yes. Bluetooth page turners like AirTurn and Donner are supported for hands-free control.
