Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

Overview

Vis-A-Verse ("the App") is a lyrics teleprompter designed for performing musicians. Your privacy is simple: we collect nothing.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All songs, setlists, and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage only. No data leaves your device.

No Accounts

The App does not require or support user accounts, logins, or registration of any kind.

No Analytics

The App does not include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry software. No usage data is collected.

No Network Requests

The App functions entirely offline. It does not make network requests to any server. The optional setlist sharing feature encodes data in a URL that you share directly — no server is involved.

No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
